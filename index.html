About

I am a Ph.D. student in Software Engineering at Xidian University, advised by Prof. Lu Wang. I entered Xidian through a combined M.S.–Ph.D. program, and during my master's phase I worked with Prof. Pengfei Yang.

My current research centers on the efficiency of large language models and agent systems — efficient LLM inference, agent system efficiency, agent memory systems, and the AI infrastructure that supports them.
